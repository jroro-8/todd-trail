# The Todd Trail

An Oregon Trail–style survival game set in St. Augustine, FL. About 20 minutes of play.

**Part 1: The Prep (14 days).** Todd is sure the grid is going down. Each day he picks one thing to do: work a shift for DJ, shop at Publix, Home Depot or the gun shop, practice at the range, mount solar panels, set up the ham radio to reach Matt, keep Kirsten happy, or learn bike repair and water purification from Jennifer. Stock up on white cake.

**Part 2: The Trail (55 miles).** The grid goes down. Lead Kirsten, Jennifer, DJ (and his hat) and Jon Galluzzo across town to the one place with generators and hoagies: **the Wawa on US-1**. The route runs past Publix, the outlets, the Fountain of Youth, the Castillo, St. George Street, the Bridge of Lions (stuck open), the Alligator Farm, the Lighthouse and the SR-312 bridge. On the way you hunt, trade, rest, ration and survive random events.

## Run it
It's a static site with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or deploy the folder to Vercel as-is.

## Files
- `index.html`: the page shell
- `style.css`: retro CRT styling
- `game.js`: the game engine, all story content, the pixel art and the hunting minigame

Saves and the Wawa Hall of Fame are kept in the browser's localStorage.
